DESIGN CHOICES
After Hours builds every letter on pointed Gothic lancet arches, high-contrast broad-nib strokes, and 45° blackletter crowns, then hacks those manuscript bones with aligned circuit-board stem notches (locked to a single horizon line across words) and faceted diamond-LED punctuation. Rather than shipping one fussy decorative wordmark, the family splits into two working OpenType masters: an expressive Display cut for dark-electric hero banners and a calmer, wider-counter Heading cut stripped of micro-cuts so 16–24px titles stay crisp on a phone screen. Every curve and contour was engineered from scratch as original cubic-Bezier geometry with zero tracing of existing typefaces.
WORKING .OTF COVERAGE & STATUS
Included Files: AfterHours-Display.otf & AfterHours-Heading.otf (+ .woff2)
Supported Characters (85 glyphs): Basic Latin A–Z, a–z, 0–9, space, and core punctuation (!?.,:;-—/&+#@()[]'"_*), plus GPOS pair kerning.
INCOMPLETE EXTENDED LATIN: Accented diacritics (é, ü, ñ) & non-Latin scripts not included in v1.0.
