2. DESIGN CHOICES (3 SENTENCES)
======================================================================
After Hours builds every letter on pointed Gothic lancet arches, high-contrast broad-nib strokes, and 45-degree blackletter crowns, then hacks those manuscript bones with aligned circuit-board stem notches (locked to a single y=420u horizon line across words) and faceted diamond-LED punctuation. Rather than shipping one fussy decorative wordmark, the family splits into two working OpenType masters: an expressive Display cut (3.35:1 contrast) for dark-electric hero banners and a calmer, wider-counter Heading cut (1.81:1 contrast, +8% wider counters, zero micro-cuts) so 16-24px titles stay crisp on a phone screen. Every curve and contour was engineered from scratch as original cubic-Bezier geometry with zero tracing of any existing typeface.

3. CHARACTER SUPPORT & EXPLICIT INCOMPLETE-FONT LABEL
======================================================================
- Supported Characters (85 glyphs per .otf file):
  * Uppercase Basic Latin: A-Z (26 glyphs)
  * Lowercase Basic Latin: a-z (26 glyphs)
  * Lining Numerals: 0-9 (10 glyphs)
  * Core Punctuation & Symbols: ! ? . , : ; - — _ / + & # @ ( ) [ ] ' " * (21 glyphs)
  * Space + .notdef (2 glyphs) + OpenType GPOS pair kerning (35 pairs incl. AF, AT, FA, FT, TE, ER, HO, OU, UR, RS)
- INCOMPLETE EXTENDED LATIN LABEL:
  This v1.0 release is complete for English / Basic Latin display & web headings, but does NOT yet include accented/extended Latin diacritics (e.g., e-acute, u-umlaut, n-tilde), currency signs, or non-Latin scripts.
